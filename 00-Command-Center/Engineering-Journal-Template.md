# Engineering Journal Template

Five to ten minutes at shutdown. The journal is where bugs, insights and half-formed ideas go before they're lost. It's also the raw material for the [[Weekly-Review]], interview stories and blog posts.

One file per month (`Journal/YYYY-MM.md`), newest entry on top.

## Entry

### YYYY-MM-DD

**Worked on:** (link the artifact or commit)

**What broke and why:**
- Symptom:
- Root cause:
- Fix:
- How I'd find it faster next time:

**Learned:** (one idea in your own words, not copied)

**Open questions:** (things to look up; move to a note when answered)
- [ ]

**Promote?**
- [ ] Bug worth a root-cause note
- [ ] Insight worth a concept note
- [ ] Result worth the [[Achievement-Log-Template|achievement log]]
- [ ] Choice worth the [[Decision-Log-Template|decision log]]

## Prompts When Stuck

- What took longer than expected today, and what was the actual cause?
- What did I assume that turned out wrong?
- What would I tell myself this morning?
- What did I do manually that I've now done three times?
