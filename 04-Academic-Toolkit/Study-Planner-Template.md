## Allocation Rule

1. Fixed items first: classes, labs, revision slot (20-30 min daily).
2. Hard deadlines within 7 days.
3. Courses at risk: at least 3 blocks each.
4. Exam prep for exams within 21 days.
5. Remaining blocks: the course with the highest credits × difficulty.

Leave about 20% of blocks unplanned as buffer.
