## Rules

- Attempt from memory first. Check the answer only after writing yours.
- For a wrong answer, write the reason in the notes: concept gap, careless error, or time.
- Tag questions that appeared in 2+ past papers with `★` in the ID column.
- Before exams, filter to ★ questions and anything not yet ✓ twice.

## Pre-Exam Checklist

- [ ] All ★ questions attempted at least twice
- [ ] No ✗ questions left from high-weight units
- [ ] Every question type in the paper pattern practised at least once under time
