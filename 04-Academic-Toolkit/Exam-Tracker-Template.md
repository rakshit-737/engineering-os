## Exam-Day Checklist

- [ ] Admit card / ID
- [ ] Pens, pencil, calculator (allowed model), instruments
- [ ] Reached venue 20 minutes early
- [ ] First 5 minutes: read the whole paper, mark easy questions
- [ ] Time budget per question = marks × (total minutes / total marks)
- [ ] 10 minutes kept at the end to review
