# Revision Checklist

Use in the last 3-5 days before an exam. Copy once per exam.

Course: · Exam date:

## Coverage

- [ ] Every syllabus unit listed with a confidence score (1-5)
- [ ] High-frequency topics from [[PYQ-Analysis-Template|PYQ analysis]] all at confidence 4+
- [ ] Topics at confidence 1-2 have either a plan or a conscious decision to skip
- [ ] No unit left completely untouched (partial marks are easier to get than you think)

## Active Recall

- [ ] Blank-page recall done for each unit
- [ ] All cue questions / flashcards for the course reviewed
- [ ] Every definition, theorem and formula stated from memory at least once
- [ ] Derivations and proofs that appeared in PYQs reproduced without notes

## Practice

- [ ] At least 2 full past papers done under time
- [ ] All wrong answers from mocks re-attempted a day later
- [ ] [[Question-Bank-Template|Question bank]] items marked "failed" re-tested
- [ ] Numerical problems: units and significant figures checked

## Condensed Material

- [ ] One-page formula / key-facts sheet written by hand
- [ ] List of your own common mistakes read
- [ ] Diagrams you'll need to draw practised

## Logistics

- [ ] Exam time, venue and allowed materials confirmed
- [ ] Calculator (allowed model) with fresh batteries
- [ ] Admit card / ID ready
- [ ] Plan for the night before: light review only, sleep 7+ hours

## Day Before

- [ ] Formula sheet and mistakes list only; no new topics
- [ ] Bag packed
