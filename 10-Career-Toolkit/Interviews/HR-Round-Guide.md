# HR Round Guide

The HR round checks fit, motivation, communication, logistics and red flags. It can reject you, so prepare it like a technical round.

## Common questions and answer frames

| Question | Frame |
|---|---|
| Tell me about yourself | Present (what you study/do) -> past (1-2 proof points) -> future (why this role). 60-90 seconds |
| Why this company? | One specific thing about their product/team/tech + how it connects to your work. Research beforehand |
| Why this role? | Link to projects that show you already do this work |
| Strengths / weaknesses | Strength with evidence; a real weakness plus what you are doing about it |
| Where do you see yourself in 5 years? | Growth in the role's direction; no "running my own startup next year" |
| Why should we hire you? | Map 2-3 JD requirements to your evidence |
| Are you open to relocation / shifts / bond? | Answer honestly; clarify terms before committing |
| Other offers / processes? | Honest and brief; do not bluff |
| Any questions for us? | Always have two: team work, onboarding, what success looks like in 6 months |

## Compensation and offers

- If asked for expectations early, you can ask for the range for the role first.
- Research typical ranges from reliable sources for your location and role; do not quote numbers you cannot justify.
- Get the offer in writing: base, variable, joining bonus, stock/vesting, bond/clawback terms, location, start date.
- Ask for the decision deadline; it is reasonable to request time to compare.
- Check your college placement policy (some restrict accepting/declining offers).

## Red flags to avoid

- Badmouthing past teams, college or interviewers.
- Inconsistency with your resume.
- No knowledge of what the company does.
