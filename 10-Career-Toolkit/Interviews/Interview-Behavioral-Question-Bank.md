# Interview Behavioral Question Bank

Method: [[Behavioral-STAR-Method]]. Store finished stories in [[STAR-Story-Template]]. For each question, jot which banked story answers it; do not write a new story per question.

## STAR prompts (use for every question)

- **S:** Where, when, who, what was at stake? (2 sentences)
- **T:** What were *you* responsible for? What did success look like?
- **A:** 3-5 steps you took. One decision with alternatives and why you chose.
- **R:** What changed, measured honestly? What did you learn / do differently now?

## Questions by theme

### Teamwork and conflict
- [ ] Tell me about a disagreement with a teammate. -> Story: ____
- [ ] Tell me about working with someone difficult. -> Story: ____
- [ ] Tell me about a time you received critical feedback. -> Story: ____

### Ownership and initiative
- [ ] Tell me about something you did beyond your assigned scope. -> Story: ____
- [ ] Tell me about a time you noticed a problem nobody else was fixing. -> Story: ____

### Failure and learning
- [ ] Tell me about a failure. -> Story: ____
- [ ] Tell me about a mistake that affected others. -> Story: ____
- [ ] Tell me about a time you had to learn something new quickly. -> Story: ____

### Ambiguity and pressure
- [ ] Tell me about a time requirements were unclear. -> Story: ____
- [ ] Tell me about a tight deadline. -> Story: ____
- [ ] Tell me about juggling competing priorities. -> Story: ____

### Leadership and influence
- [ ] Tell me about leading without formal authority. -> Story: ____
- [ ] Tell me about convincing others of a technical decision. -> Story: ____

### Technical judgment
- [ ] Your most challenging technical problem. -> Story: ____
- [ ] A tradeoff you made and would make differently now. -> Story: ____
- [ ] A time you simplified something complex. -> Story: ____

### Security-specific (if applicable)
- [ ] A time you found a vulnerability or risky practice and how you reported it. -> Story: ____
- [ ] A time you balanced security against usability or delivery. -> Story: ____

## Follow-ups to expect

"What exactly did you do?" · "What would you do differently?" · "How did the other person react?" · "How do you know it worked?"

If you have no real story for a question, adapt the nearest real one honestly. Never invent events.
